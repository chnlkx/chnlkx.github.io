Research

2024–2026 · XENONnT

Listening for solar neutrinos

I led the solar boron-8 neutrino analysis and the search for light dark matter, working on low-energy event reconstruction, background modeling and statistical inference. Across three science runs, we measured solar coherent elastic neutrino–nucleus scattering (CEνNS).

Measurement significance
3.3σ
Tonne-year exposure
6.77
Read the 2026 PRL paper

2023–2024

First indication in XENONnT

I led the search that yielded the first indication of solar-neutrino CEνNS in a dark-matter detector (2.73σ). The result was featured in APS Physics’ 2024 highlights.

2021–2023

Accidental coincidences

I built a data-driven background model and event selections that suppressed accidental coincidences by three orders of magnitude. The framework supported XENONnT’s WIMP and solar-neutrino searches.

2021–2025

Liquid xenon detector R&D

I measured radon-daughter plate-out on XENONnT electrodes and evaluated surface treatments. At Tsinghua (2021–2023), I designed and operated the university’s first full-function liquid xenon circulation and purification system.
